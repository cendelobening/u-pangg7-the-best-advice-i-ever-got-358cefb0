Entry for the make.fun bounty "The Best Advice I Ever Got": a single
sentence, at most 200 characters, about the best advice I ever received.
It is the advice my father gave me on the day I moved out — plain,
practical, and quietly devastating once you have lived it.

> My dad handed me my suitcase and said: never lend money to a friend you want to keep, because the debt outlives the friendship and you'll lose both.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the sentence to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default
`/output/entry.txt`) with mode `0644`.
